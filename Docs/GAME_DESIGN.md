# Game Design — Realms of the Shattered Crown

## Vision
An original single-player retro fantasy RPG inspired by the
exploration and adventure structure of early computer RPGs.

## Core experience
Explore a kingdom, visit settlements, discover dungeons,
fight monsters, collect treasure, improve equipment and
complete quests.

## Presentation
### Overworld
- Angled top-down camera.
- Tile-based movement.
- Terrain includes grass, forests, mountains and water.
- Towns, landmarks and dungeon entrances.

### Dungeons
- First-person pseudo-3D perspective.
- Grid-based movement and turning.
- Corridors, walls, doors, treasure and monsters.
- Begin with a fixed, handcrafted test map.

## RPG systems
- Health and basic character statistics.
- Turn-based combat.
- Gold, equipment and inventory.
- Keys, locked doors and quests.
- Save and load.

## First playable quest
The player explores a small overworld and enters a dungeon.
Inside, the player defeats a skeleton, finds a silver key,
opens a locked room and retrieves a crown.
Returning to town completes the quest and awards gold.

## Scope limits
The first prototype does not include multiplayer,
procedural world generation, crafting, complex dialogue,
or a large open world.

## Art direction
Use simple placeholder visuals initially.
Create original characters, locations, names and assets.
